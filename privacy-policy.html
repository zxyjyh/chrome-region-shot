隐私政策 — Region Shot · 区域截图

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日　·　最后更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日

一句话：这个扩展只把你在网页上框选的那块区域，在你自己的电脑上拼成一张 PNG 图片，然后存到你的下载文件夹。

没有任何数据被上传、存储到服务器、分享给第三方或用于广告。

即使这些处理全部在你的设备本地完成，我们也按下面各节完整披露处理了什么、为什么处理、以及不处理什么。

1. 这个扩展做什么

Region Shot 是一个截图工具。你在网页上点击扩展图标后，拖拽确定截图区域的起点和宽度，滚动到想结束的位置，点确认，扩展会把这段跨越多屏的内容截成一张完整的 PNG 图片并保存到本地。

它没有其他功能。这是它在 Chrome 应用商店里申报的单一用途（single purpose）。

2. 我们处理哪些数据

2.1 网页内容（截取区域内的画面）

当你完成选区并确认后，扩展会读取选区覆盖范围内的网页渲染画面，用于生成你要的那张截图。这属于 Chrome 应用商店定义的「网站内容（Website content）」。读取范围严格限制在你框选的矩形内，不会读取选区之外的页面内容。

这些像素数据只在你的浏览器内存里存在，用于拼接画面，图片生成后即释放。

2.2 页面几何信息与滚动位置

为了让跨多屏截图对齐，扩展需要知道：选区在页面中的坐标、当前滚动容器滚到了哪里、可视区域有多大。这些信息从当前页面即时读取，仅用于计算下一屏该截哪里，不写入任何存储、不发送到任何地方。

2.3 框选过程中的操作信息

在你框选和滚动定位的阶段，扩展会响应鼠标按下、移动、松开和页面滚动事件，用来画选框、跟随滚动延伸选区。这些事件只用于界面即时反馈，不记录、不统计、不上报。

2.4 我们不处理的数据

数据类型是否处理
浏览历史（访问过哪些网站、网址列表）不处理。扩展没有申请 history 权限，不读取、不记录任何地址
表单中输入的内容、密码、支付信息不处理。除非你主动把某一区域框进了截图，否则不会被读取
身份信息（姓名、邮箱、账号、手机号）不处理。扩展不登录、不申请任何身份相关权限
设备标识符、指纹不处理
地理位置不处理
剪贴板不处理
使用统计、崩溃报告、埋点不存在。代码中没有任何分析 SDK 或统计上报

3. 数据如何被使用

只有一个用途：生成你要的那张截图。具体过程是：扩展控制页面逐屏滚动，每屏截取选区范围内的画面，在浏览器后台把多张画面拼接成一张完整图片，然后保存为 PNG 文件。

截图开始前，扩展会记录当前的滚动位置；截图完成后会把页面滚动位置恢复回去。截图期间你会看到页面自动上下滚动，这是功能的固有行为，不是采集行为。

4. 数据存储与保留

  • 扩展本身不存储任何数据。它没有使用 chrome.storage、localStorage、IndexedDB 或 Cookie。卸载扩展不会丢失任何属于你的东西，因为它本来就没存。
  • 截图过程中的画面数据只存在于内存中，图片生成后即被释放。
  • 最终生成的 PNG 文件保存在你自己的下载文件夹，由你自行决定保留、移动或删除。扩展不会再次读取它。

5. 数据是否传输到外部

不传输。扩展代码中不存在任何向外部服务器发送数据的网络请求——没有 fetch 到远程地址、没有 XMLHttpRequest、没有 WebSocket、没有打点上报。（代码中唯一一处 fetch 调用，读取的是浏览器内存里的本地 data: URL，用于在下载接口失败时兜底保存文件，不产生任何网络流量。）

6. 数据共享与出售

不共享，不出售。扩展不包含任何第三方 SDK、广告组件、统计工具或外部分析服务。我们不会把数据转让给广告平台、数据经纪商或任何其他第三方。

7. 权限说明

扩展申请了四项权限，每一项都只服务于「截图」这一个用途：

权限为什么需要
activeTab 只在你主动点击扩展图标或按下快捷键时，临时获得当前这一个标签页的访问权。不使用常驻的站点访问权限，不会在你浏览时后台读取任何页面。
scripting 把框选界面注入当前页面，并在截图过程中控制页面滚动位置。
downloads 把生成好的 PNG 文件保存到你的下载文件夹。
debugger 调用 Chrome 调试协议（CDP）的截图接口读取页面画面。详见下一节。

8. 关于 debugger 权限的特别说明

这是四项权限里最需要解释的一项，所以我们单独说明它具体做了什么、以及没做什么。

  • 什么时候附加：只在你点击扩展图标、开始截图之后。不是常驻附加，不会在你日常浏览时挂在页面上。
  • 附加期间会做什么：只发送一条命令 —— Page.captureScreenshot，也就是「把当前页面画面给我」。除此之外不读取 DOM、不读取网络请求、不读取 Cookie、不读取表单内容、不执行 JavaScript、不修改页面。
  • 什么时候断开：截图结束立即断开，无论成功还是失败都会断开（代码中放在 finally 块里）。
  • 你会看到什么：附加期间 Chrome 会在顶部显示一条「正在调试此浏览器」的提示条，截图结束自动消失。这条提示是 Chrome 的安全机制，不是扩展自己的界面。你的浏览器不需要开启「开发者模式」。
  • 做不到的事：它不能在你不知情时附加，不能跨标签页截图，不能读取未选中的区域。
  • 为什么需要它：扩展自带的 tabs.captureVisibleTab 只能截当前可见的一屏，且无法在程序化滚动后保证每次截取的画面尺寸一致；跨多屏拼接需要更精确的分屏控制。CDP 是 Chrome 提供的官方接口，只在你授权的情况下可用。

如果某个页面已经打开了 DevTools，Chrome 不允许扩展同时附加调试器，此时扩展会显示失败标记 ! 并自动退出。

9. 你的控制权

  • 随时在 chrome://extensions 里卸载扩展，所有功能立即停止。
  • 截图过程中按 Esc 或点右键即可取消，不会生成任何文件。
  • 已经保存到下载文件夹的图片完全由你掌控，扩展不会回读、不会删除、不会上传。
  • 不想要调试提示条？不启动截图就不会附加调试器。

English Version

Privacy Policy — Region Shot

Effective date: November 17, 2026　·　Last updated: November 17, 2026

In one sentence: this extension stitches the area you select on a web page into a single PNG image, on your own computer, and saves it to your Downloads folder.

No data is uploaded, stored on a server, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Even though all processing happens locally on your device, the sections below disclose in full what we process, why, and what we do not process.

1. What this extension does

Region Shot is a screenshot tool. After you click the extension icon on a web page, you drag to set the starting point and width of a capture region, scroll to the point where the capture should end, and confirm. The extension then captures that multi-screen region as one complete PNG image and saves it locally.

It has no other functionality. This is the single purpose declared to the Chrome Web Store.

2. What data we process

2.1 Website content (the pixels inside your selection)

After you confirm a selection, the extension reads the rendered appearance of the web page within your selected rectangle in order to produce the screenshot. This is "Website content" as defined by the Chrome Web Store. Reading is strictly limited to the rectangle you selected; content outside it is not read.

These pixel values exist only in your browser's memory while the image is being stitched, and are released once the file is generated.

2.2 Page geometry and scroll position

To align a capture that spans multiple screens, the extension needs to know the coordinates of the selection on the page, where the current scroll container is scrolled to, and how large the visible viewport is. This information is read from the current page at the moment it is needed, used solely to compute the next capture segment, and is never written to storage or transmitted anywhere.

2.3 Interaction events during selection

While you drag and scroll to define the capture region, the extension responds to mouse press, move, release, and page scroll events in order to draw the selection box and extend it as you scroll. These events drive immediate interface feedback only; they are not logged, measured, or reported.

2.4 What we do not process

Data typeProcessed?
Browsing history (which sites you visited, list of URLs)No. The extension does not request the history permission and reads no addresses.
Form entries, passwords, payment detailsNo, unless you deliberately include a region containing them in a screenshot.
Identity information (name, email, account, phone)No. There is no sign-in and no identity-related permission.
Device identifiers or fingerprintsNo.
LocationNo.
ClipboardNo.
Usage analytics, crash reports, telemetryNone. There is no analytics SDK and no reporting code of any kind.

3. How data is used

For one purpose only: producing the screenshot you asked for. The extension scrolls the page one screen at a time, captures the selected area on each screen, stitches the screens into a single image in the background, and saves it as a PNG file.

Before capturing, the extension records the current scroll position and restores it when finished. You will see the page scroll by itself during the capture; this is inherent to how the feature works, not a form of collection.

4. Storage and retention

  • The extension stores no data. It does not use chrome.storage, localStorage, IndexedDB, or cookies. Uninstalling it loses nothing of yours, because nothing of yours was ever stored.
  • In-progress image data exists only in memory and is released once the PNG is generated.
  • The resulting PNG is saved to your own Downloads folder. You decide whether to keep, move, or delete it. The extension never reads it again.

5. Transmission to external servers

None. The extension contains no network requests that send data anywhere — no remote fetch, no XMLHttpRequest, no WebSocket, no telemetry endpoint. (The only fetch call in the code reads a local data: URL held in browser memory, used as a fallback for saving the file when the downloads API fails. It generates no network traffic.)

6. Sharing and sale of data

Not shared, not sold. The extension contains no third-party SDKs, ad components, analytics tools, or external services. We do not transfer data to advertising platforms, data brokers, or any other third party.

7. Permissions

8. About the debugger permission

This is the most powerful permission we request, so here is exactly what it does and does not do.

  • When it attaches: only after you click the extension icon and start a capture. It is never attached persistently and is never attached while you browse normally.
  • What it does while attached: it sends exactly one command — Page.captureScreenshot, meaning "give me the rendered image of the current page." It does not read the DOM, network requests, cookies, or form values; it does not execute JavaScript; it does not modify the page.
  • When it detaches: immediately when the capture ends, on success and on failure alike (the detach call sits in a finally block).
  • What you will see: while attached, Chrome displays a "being debugged" banner at the top of the browser. It disappears when the capture finishes. This banner is Chrome's own safety indicator, not part of the extension's UI. You do not need to enable Developer Mode.
  • What it cannot do: it cannot attach without your action, cannot capture other tabs, and cannot read anything outside your selection.
  • Why it is necessary: the built-in tabs.captureVisibleTab captures only the currently visible screen and cannot guarantee consistent capture dimensions across programmatic scrolls; accurate multi-screen stitching requires finer control over per-screen capture. The DevTools Protocol is Chrome's official interface for this and is available only with your authorization.

If a page already has DevTools open, Chrome does not allow the extension to attach a debugger. In that case the extension shows a failure badge ! and exits without producing a file.

9. Your controls

  • Uninstall the extension at any time from chrome://extensions; all functionality stops immediately.
  • Press Esc or right-click during a capture to cancel; no file is produced.
  • Images already saved to your Downloads folder are entirely under your control; the extension never reads, deletes, or uploads them.
  • Do not want the debug banner? If you never start a capture, the debugger is never attached.

10. Children's privacy

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their personal information. Because it collects and transmits no data, we neither can nor need to identify a user's age.
